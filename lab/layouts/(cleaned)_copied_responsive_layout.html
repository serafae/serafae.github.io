I’m Serafae.

Offline, I’m just Dan.

Serafae is a name I created — a fusion of Serenity, the Moon Princess, and Bloom, Fairy of the Dragon Flame.

These two heroines shaped how I understand transformation, sacrifice, and becoming someone new without losing the self underneath. Serenity gave me softness and lunar compassion; Bloom gave me fire, wings, and the courage to fight for a home even in ruins.

Together, they form a name that carries light and heat, gentleness and strength, magic and resolve.

This is my personal NeoCities site and the only official place I exist online — no social media, no other platforms. I’ve been building here for over two years now.

This place is Magical Girl crossed with Nu-Metal. Soft lace and pastel rainbow sparkles against a dark blue base, butterfly wings that still carry an edge, neon glow over gothic shadows.

I blend the things people usually keep separate: hyperfeminine aesthetic and technical systems, fashion and code, glitter and distortion.


(And That Is) My Prerogative!

I made this space as a digital bedroom and dream‑realm for the things I actually care about: Magical Girl transformation stories, Nu‑Metal energy, lace, butterflies, old‑web charm, and the freedom to mix softness with sharp edges without apology.

This site is under construction and always will be. That is intentional. It grows the way I grow — slowly, deliberately, and with room to change.

What comes next: original work, personal projects, inspired pieces, artwork, and old‑web content. Things that belong to me. Things that carry the atmosphere I care about.

This is where they will live.


No External Affiliations:
