Atabook Etiquette & Guidelines

Welcome to my Guestbook!

This guestbook is here for visitors to leave friendly messages, comments, and positive interactions related to the website.



Be respectful

Please treat this space and its visitors with respect. Harassment, personal attacks, hateful remarks, discrimination, threats, or attempts to upset others are not allowed.

Keep messages appropriate

This is a public guestbook. Please avoid excessive profanity, explicit content, inappropriate remarks, graphic material, or posts that are intended to make others uncomfortable.

No spam or disruption

Do not post spam, advertisements, repetitive messages, trolling, or content meant only to disrupt the community.

Respect the site's atmosphere

This website is a personal creative space, not a place for arguments, political debates, or hostile discussions. Please keep messages relevant, friendly, and in good faith.

Respect moderation

Some language may be automatically filtered to help maintain a welcoming environment. Attempts to bypass filters or intentionally post prohibited content may result in messages being removed or access being restricted.

Help keep this space enjoyable

Everyone who visits this site should be able to enjoy it without dealing with hostility or inappropriate behavior. Please use good judgment when leaving a message.

Messages that do not follow these guidelines may be removed, and repeated violations may result in restricted access.
